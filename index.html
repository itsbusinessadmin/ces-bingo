Game Guide

How to Play

1. Join before the first ball.

Players enter through the shared link or QR code, choose their cards, and must finish joining before the Game Master rolls the first number. Once the first number is drawn, the room is locked to new players.

2. Tap the called numbers.

Every number revealed by the Game Master is synced to all players. Players tap it on their cards; if they don't within 5 seconds, it is marked automatically. Uncalled numbers cannot be marked.

3. Bingo is detected automatically.

As soon as a player's marked numbers complete an approved pattern, Bingo is claimed and verified against the called numbers. No manual claim is required.

4. Winners update live.

When a player completes a valid pattern, all players see the winner announcement and the Game Master screen lists the winners in order.

5. Reset starts a choice period.

After a round is reset, existing players can stay with the same cards, add more cards, or leave the game. The next first ball locks new entries again.

Any one completed pattern below counts as BINGO. The center FREE space counts automatically when it is part of a pattern.
